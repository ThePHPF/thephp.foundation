Our mission

How we work toward our mission

Read our announcement for more information about the inspiration behind the launch of The PHP Foundation.

Financial Support for Core Developers

The PHP Foundation provides financial support and guidance to PHP language developers who improve the language in the areas of high-quality maintenance, security, and the implementation of new features approved through the PHP community's RFC process.

Community Growth

In the interest of retaining existing and gaining new contributors and users, The PHP Foundation aims to:

  • promote the public image of the PHP language
  • improve the onboarding experience for new users
  • encourage the prioritization of accessibility and inclusion in the PHP community

PHP Representation

The PHP Foundation also represents the interests of the PHP language and community in open source policy discussions and collaborative partnerships across the open source landscape.

Our work

Real work on PHP, every day

The foundation contracts a team of part- and full-time developers who work on maintenance, security, and new features for PHP.

The foundation team contributes in many forms: filing issues, reviewing pull requests, participating in discussions on mailing lists, triaging issues, submitting RFC proposals, writing documentation, maintaining project infrastructure, fixing bugs, and implementing features.
