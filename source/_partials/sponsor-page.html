How it works

How yearly and monthly contributions count

  • As soon as your total contributions pass a level's threshold, through one donation or monthly payments added up, you become a sponsor at that level.
  • Sponsors are removed from the list at the end of each calendar year if their total contributions for that year are below the threshold for their level.

For example, if you contribute $1,000 monthly starting in January, your total reaches $12,000 in December. That makes you a Silver member for the rest of the year and all of the next.

{# ───────────── Deck + FAQ ───────────── #}

Common questions

Is a small amount worth it?

Yes. A lot of PHP development is paid for by many small contributions. $100 a month from a few hundred companies keeps several engineers working full time.

Who do we actually pay?

The PHP Foundation operates under Open Source Collective, which handles banking, taxes and legal work. Payments go through Open Collective. Official info & docs.

Can we get an invoice instead of paying by card?

Yes. Email us your company details and the amount, and we'll send an invoice you can pay by bank transfer.

Is this tax-deductible?

No. Our fiscal host, Open Source Collective, is a US 501(c)(6) non-profit, so contributions are not charitable donations. Most companies book them as a business expense or sponsorship. Please check with your accountant how this applies in your country.

How is the money spent?

Mostly on paying core developers to maintain PHP, fix security issues and build new features. All expenses are public.

Will our logo be shown?

Companies reaching Silver, Gold or Platinum get their logo on our website. Every contributor is listed on our Open Collective page.
