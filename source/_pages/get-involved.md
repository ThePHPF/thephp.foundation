The PHP Foundation exists to support, advance, and develop the PHP language. It's an organization with a budget and donors, but the vast majority of its effort comes from the work of volunteer members of the community.

PHP thrives when we all take part, and there are many ways to contribute, whether you write code or not. Below are the main opportunities to get involved, from our Special Interest Groups and community Discord to contributing to PHP itself and supporting the Foundation financially.

Whether you're an individual developer, a company that runs PHP, a community organizer, or any other kind of PHP community member, we'd love to include you.

## Join a Special Interest Group (SIG)

Special Interest Groups are the primary way community members can participate in the work of the Foundation. Each group brings together community members and Foundation staff to tackle a documented gap in the PHP ecosystem, and most of the work happens out in the open, so you can jump in regardless of where you are in the world or how much time you have.

**The easiest way to start** is to fill out the [general SIG interest form](https://forms.gle/PqNRftrMGAhMCWBe9) and let us know which group (or groups) you'd like to join. You can also drop into the SIG chats on our community Discord (see below), or connect asynchronously through each group's GitHub repository.

We've launched six SIGs. You're welcome in more than one.

### Ecosystem Security Team

Works to improve the security of the PHP ecosystem and help maintainers as AI-assisted vulnerability research becomes more common, by proactively identifying vulnerabilities, triaging reports, building tooling, and scanning projects across the ecosystem.

- **Get involved:** Say hi in the `#ecosystem-security` channel on the [#phpc Discord](https://discord.phpc.social/), or email [volker@thephp.foundation](mailto:volker@thephp.foundation) if you'd like a scan or have questions.
- **Read more:** [Announcing the Ecosystem Security Team](https://thephp.foundation/blog/2026/05/18/announcing-ecosystem-security-team/)

### PHP Ambassador Program

Advocacy for PHP *outside* the PHP bubble. Ambassadors help tell the story of modern PHP—at local meetups, inside companies, and at open-source conferences—with shared talk assets, a "State of Modern PHP" deck, and CfP coordination. It's organized into three subgroups: **Research**, **Marketing**, and **Speakers**.

- **Get involved:** Fill out the [interest form](https://forms.gle/amMY3Anvh83jUE4Q6), join the conversation in `#php-ambassadors` (plus `#php-amb-research`, `#php-amb-marketing`, and `#php-amb-speakers`) on the [#phpc Discord](https://discord.phpc.social/), and contribute via the [SIG-ambassadors repo](https://github.com/ThePHPF/SIG-ambassadors).
- **Read more:** [The PHP Ambassador Program is Open](https://thephp.foundation/blog/2026/06/19/the-php-ambassador-program-is-open/) · [PHP Ambassadors, six weeks in](https://thephp.foundation/blog/2026/08/13/php-ambassadors-six-weeks-in/)

### PHP Onboarding Initiative

Improves the experience of new PHP users from first encounter through first deployment—identifying setup pain points, coordinating learning resources, documenting a clear "getting started" path, and exploring mentorship.

- **Get involved:** Register your interest with [this form](https://forms.gle/VNmijQm4gWjuDjnJA), join `#the-php-foundation` on the [#phpc Discord](https://discord.phpc.social/) for access to the SIG chats, and connect asynchronously in the [SIG-onboarding repo](https://github.com/ThePHPF/SIG-onboarding).
- **Read more:** [Kicking off the PHP Onboarding Initiative](https://thephp.foundation/blog/2026/08/03/kicking-off-the-php-onboarding-initiative/)

### Cryptography Special Interest Group

A venue for Post-Quantum Cryptography readiness and related cryptography conversations in PHP—mapping where cryptography lives across PHP core and the ecosystem, tracking upstream developments in OpenSSL and libsodium, and working toward a public readiness roadmap.

- **Get involved:** Let us know through the [general SIG interest form](https://forms.gle/PqNRftrMGAhMCWBe9).

### Community Events Coalition

Connects user group leaders, meetup hosts, and conference organizers so they can tackle shared challenges—declining attendance, rising costs, finding sponsors and speakers—together. It also serves as a resource for anyone thinking of starting a local user group or conference.

- **Get involved:** Complete the [SIG interest form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSebw6h2OE-yvkUUkZsvPmYaix8WxHgmeFsZB16mi83-cAKFFA/viewform?usp=header) and join us on the [#phpc Discord](https://discord.phpc.social/).
- **Read more:** [Kicking Off the Community Events Coalition](https://thephp.foundation/blog/2026/09/28/kicking-off-the-community-events-coalition-sig/)

### PHP Accessibility & Inclusion Special Interest Group

Works to make the language, its documentation, and its learning resources more accessible across abilities, geographies, languages, and career stages—and supports maintainers in building healthy, inclusive communities, including help drafting codes of conduct.

- **Get involved:** Register through the [general SIG interest form](https://forms.gle/PqNRftrMGAhMCWBe9).

For the bigger picture on why these groups exist and what each is working toward, read our [2026 strategy document](https://thephp.foundation/blog/2026/06/11/integrating-community-feedback-into-foundation-strategy-part2/).

## Join us on the #phpc Discord

Much of the Foundation's day-to-day community conversation happens on the [#phpc Discord server](https://discord.phpc.social/), where the [PHP Community Foundation](https://phpcommunity.org) hosts channels dedicated to our work:

- `#the-php-foundation` — the general Foundation channel and your gateway to the SIG chats
- `#ecosystem-security` — security discussion with the Ecosystem Security Team
- `#php-ambassadors`, `#php-amb-research`, `#php-amb-marketing`, `#php-amb-speakers` — the PHP Ambassador Program and its subgroups

New to the server? Use the [invite link](https://discord.com/invite/RYajXKxuuK) to join, then say hello.

## Contribute to PHP itself

You don't need to be a C developer to contribute to PHP. There are many ways to help, both inside PHP core and across the wider ecosystem:

- **Test prereleases** against your own applications and report what breaks.
- **Report and reproduce bugs**, or confirm existing ones.
- **Improve documentation** on [php.net](https://php.net/docs).
- **Write tests** to increase coverage of the language.
- **Fix or review issues** in [php-src](https://github.com/php/php-src).
- **Participate in internals** discussion and the RFC process.
- **Help existing open-source projects**, teach and mentor developers, and strengthen your local PHP communities.

For a thorough, friendly walkthrough of each of these—and where to turn when you get stuck—see our guide: [How to contribute to PHP](https://thephp.foundation/blog/2026/08/17/how-to-contribute-to-php/).
You will find more practical advice for contributors and maintainers on our [Guides](https://thephp.foundation/guides) page.

When you need to get unstuck, the [PHP Community Discord](https://phpc.chat/) has channels ranging from beginner questions (`#php-src-help`) to core development (`#php-internals`), and the [internals mailing list](https://www.php.net/mailing-lists.php) is the canonical venue for development discussion.

## Support the Foundation financially

For individuals and organizations without contributor time to spare, financial support is one of the most direct ways to help. It funds the developers and initiatives that keep PHP moving forward.

- [Become a sponsor](/sponsor) — sponsorship tiers for companies and organizations.
- Donate in USD: [opencollective.com/phpfoundation](https://opencollective.com/phpfoundation)
- Donate in EUR: [opencollective.com/php-foundation-eur](https://opencollective.com/php-foundation-eur)
